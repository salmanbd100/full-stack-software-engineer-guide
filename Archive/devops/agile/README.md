# Agile and DevOps Culture

> ⚠️ **Archived by improvement #25.** This was `DevOps/Agile/`, and it was the last directory in
> `DevOps/` — which no longer exists. What the book kept is two Part IX chapters,
> [Chapter ?? — Ways of Working](#ch-ways-of-working) and
> [Chapter ?? — Engineering Culture](#ch-engineering-culture), plus the collaboration material now in
> [Chapter ?? — Written Communication](#ch-written-communication).

Eight chapters on Scrum ceremonies, Jira workflows and team metrics is more than the topic earns in a
book about building software. Three of them carry the interview weight — what Agile is *for*, how
teams handle ownership and failure, and what the four DORA metrics do and do not measure. The rest is
tooling detail that dates faster than it teaches.
